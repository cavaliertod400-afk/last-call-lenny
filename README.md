# Last Call Lenny

Lenny got cut off at the Rusty Nail and has to stumble home, by way of a golf course, a closed mall and the desert. A side-scrolling platformer in a single HTML file.

- Drinks are power-ups: whiskey makes you big, tequila throws limes, absinthe makes you invincible, and water sobers you up. Every drink raises your blood alcohol, and you black out at 0.35.
- Four levels: the pub streets, a golf course, a closed mall and the desert. Each has its own hallucinations (gremlins, demon gophers, living mannequins, UFOs) and what they really are when Lenny briefly sobers up (security guards, groundskeepers, mall cops, vultures).
- The further Lenny gets from the pub, the more he loses his grip: more sway and double vision, motion trails, colour shifts, and shorter, rarer sober moments.
- Keyboard: arrow keys or A/D to walk, Space/↑/Z to jump, Shift/X to run or throw limes, P to pause, M to mute.
- Phones: Game Boy-style D-pad and A/B buttons. Add it to your home screen to play full screen.

Play it by opening `index.html` in a browser, or turn on GitHub Pages for this repository.

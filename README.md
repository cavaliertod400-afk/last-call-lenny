# Last Call Lenny

Lenny got cut off at the Rusty Nail and has to stumble home. A side-scrolling platformer in a single HTML file.

- Drinks are power-ups: whiskey makes you big, tequila throws limes, absinthe makes you invincible, and water sobers you up. Every drink raises your blood alcohol, and you black out at 0.35.
- Gremlins, pink elephants and bat gremlins. Every so often Lenny sobers up for a moment and sees security guards, doormen and pigeons.
- Keyboard: arrow keys or A/D to walk, Space/↑/Z to jump, Shift/X to run or throw limes, P to pause, M to mute.
- Phones: Game Boy-style D-pad and A/B buttons. Add it to your home screen to play full screen.

Play it by opening `index.html` in a browser, or turn on GitHub Pages for this repository.
